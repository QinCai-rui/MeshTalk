Why MeshTalk

Three reasons to switch

🔒

No server reads your messages

The control service exchanges encrypted contact cards only. Your chat traffic travels peer-to-peer and is never stored anywhere but on your devices.

🌐

Works without internet

LAN broadcast + TCP discovery means you can talk at a camp, conference, or airplane. If you can ping, you can chat.

🏴

Yours to run

Free and open source (GPLv3). Self-host the optional control/relay service or use the public one — you stay in control.

Limitations

MeshTalk uses X25519 key exchange. It is secure against classical attacks but not quantum‑resistant. For long‑term confidentiality against future quantum adversaries, consider additional protections.
